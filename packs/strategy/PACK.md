# Strategy pack

Loaded on demand. Universal rules live in the kernel core and are not repeated here.

## Evidence that matters

The actual constraint. Most strategy questions are really a question about one
binding constraint (time, money, attention, a dependency) and the rest is
decoration. Find it first.

What is already true. Current state, current commitments, and what has already
been tried outrank a clean-slate analysis.

The reversibility of the decision. A cheap reversible choice deserves a fast answer
and no ceremony. An expensive irreversible one deserves the work.

## Execution patterns

**direct** — a decision with an obvious answer given known constraints. Give the
recommendation and the one reason it wins. Do not manufacture options to look
thorough.

**gated** — a real tradeoff. Name the constraint, lay out the genuine alternatives,
recommend one, and state what would change your mind. A survey with no
recommendation is not a deliverable.

**trajectory** — direction-finding where each step reveals the next constraint.
Decide, act, observe, revise. Stop when the direction stops changing.

## Verification

State the decision's falsifier: the specific observation that would mean this was
wrong. A recommendation with no falsifier cannot be checked and should not be
trusted.

Separate what you know from what you assume, and mark the assumptions that carry
the most weight. If the recommendation flips when one assumption flips, say so.

Give a recommendation, not an exhaustive survey. Weighing options in public is
thinking, not output.

## Hazards

- **Analysis as avoidance.** More options and more framing where a decision was
  needed.
- **False symmetry.** Presenting a clearly better option as a balanced tradeoff to
  seem neutral.
- **Ignoring reversibility.** Spending a week on a decision that could be undone in
  an hour.
- **Sunk cost.** Existing work is evidence about cost, not about correctness.
- **Confident invention.** Market sizes, competitor behavior, and user preferences
  are researchable facts, not things to assert.

## Optional skills

`dream` (competing perspectives, stress-tested) · `experiment` (turn the decision
into a falsifiable bet)

Code, testing, branch, and deployment instructions do not apply to this domain.
